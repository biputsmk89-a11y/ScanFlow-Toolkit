1. Introduction & Core Commitment

ScanFlow ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an open, private, industrial-strength document utility. We firmly believe that documents contain personal, corporate, legal, and financial information that must remain entirely within the user's custody.

This Privacy Policy outlines how ScanFlow interacts with your files and device resources. In short: we do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal data or document contents.

3. Android Permissions Explanation

ScanFlow requests strictly minimum operating system permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used solely to allow you to select documents and images you wish to convert or edit via the system picker.
  • NO Camera Permission Required: ScanFlow is an upload-based converter and does not access your camera hardware.
  • NO Internet Permission Required: All processing, AI semantic search, OCR, and conversion tools operate completely offline.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Optional biometric authentication used solely to lock/unlock access to the application locally on supported hardware. Biometric data is managed by Android Keystore and is never accessible to ScanFlow.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

ScanFlow contains NO third-party advertising SDKs, NO analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Facebook Analytics, AppsFlyer, or Adjust), and NO data brokers. The application functions completely air-gapped without an active internet connection.

5. User Data Control & Deletion

Because ScanFlow stores all operational logs and index caches strictly in Android scoped storage (/data/user/0/com.scanflow.convertdocument/), you maintain 100% control over your data:

  • Immediate Cache Wipe: You can purge all cached thumbnails and temporary files with one click from Settings > Storage > Clear Cache.
  • Complete App Wipe: Uninstalling ScanFlow instantly and irreversibly removes all local application data, database records, and settings from your device.
