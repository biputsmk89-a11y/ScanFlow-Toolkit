Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026 • Version 1.3.1

ScanFlow Convert Document ("ScanFlow", "we", "our") is designed from the ground up as an offline-first, zero-knowledge application. We respect and protect the privacy of every user.

  • ✓ Zero Cloud Uploads: All document conversions, image processing, OCR, and AI insights happen strictly on your local device CPU.
  • ✓ No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal data, files, or usage analytics.
  • ✓ No Third-Party Advertising SDKs: ScanFlow is 100% free of advertising trackers and third-party data brokers.
  • ✓ No Camera or Invasive Permissions: ScanFlow operates purely on files you explicitly select via the standard Android document picker. No camera access or background location is requested.

Because no user data or document contents ever leave your physical device, we have no access to your files, passwords, or converted records.

For questions or open-source inquiries, visit our official repository on GitHub.
