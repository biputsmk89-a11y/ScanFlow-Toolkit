Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 • Version 1.2.0

ScanFlow — All-in-One Document Toolkit ("ScanFlow", "we", "our") is designed from the ground up as an offline-first, zero-knowledge application. We respect and protect the privacy of every user.

  • ✓ Zero Cloud Uploads: All camera capture, image processing, OCR, and PDF conversions happen strictly on your local device CPU.
  • ✓ No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal data, files, or usage analytics.
  • ✓ No Third-Party Advertising SDKs: ScanFlow is 100% free of advertising trackers and third-party data brokers.
  • ✓ Scoped Permissions: Camera access is used solely for live document scanning. Storage access is limited to files you explicitly import.

Because no user data or document contents ever leave your physical device, we have no access to your files, passwords, or scanned records.

For questions or open-source inquiries, visit our official repository on GitHub.
